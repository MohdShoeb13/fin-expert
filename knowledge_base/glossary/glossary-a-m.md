---
title: "Financial Glossary: A–M"
category: glossary
source: Compiled from Investopedia and SEC investor.gov definitions
---

**Asset allocation:** How a portfolio is divided among stocks, bonds, and cash.

**Asset class:** A category of investments with similar behavior (stocks, bonds, cash, real estate).

**Bear market:** A decline of 20%+ from a recent peak.

**Bid/Ask:** The highest price buyers will pay / lowest price sellers will accept; the gap is the spread.

**Blue chip:** A large, established, financially sound company.

**Bond:** A loan to a government or corporation paying interest and returning principal at maturity.

**Bull market:** A sustained period of rising prices.

**Capital gain:** Profit from selling an asset above its purchase price; taxed only when realized.

**Compound interest:** Earning returns on prior returns — the engine of long-term growth.

**Correction:** A market decline of 10–20%.

**Cost basis:** Original purchase price (plus reinvested dividends) used to compute taxable gains.

**Diversification:** Spreading investments to reduce the impact of any single failure.

**Dividend:** A cash distribution of company profits to shareholders.

**Dollar-cost averaging:** Investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule regardless of price.

**Duration:** A bond's sensitivity to interest-rate changes (≈ % price change per 1% rate move).

**EPS (earnings per share):** Company profit divided by share count.

**ETF:** A fund holding a basket of securities that trades on an exchange like a stock.

**Expense ratio:** A fund's annual fee as a percentage of assets.

**Fiduciary:** An advisor legally required to act in your best interest.

**Index fund:** A fund that passively tracks a market index at minimal cost.

**Inflation:** The general rise in prices that erodes cash's purchasing power.

**IPO:** Initial public offering — a company's first sale of stock to the public.

**Liquidity:** How quickly an asset converts to cash at fair value.

**Market capitalization:** Share price × shares outstanding; a company's total market value.

**Maturity:** The date a bond repays its principal.
