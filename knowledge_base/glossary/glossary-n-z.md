---
title: "Financial Glossary: N–Z"
category: glossary
source: Compiled from Investopedia and SEC investor.gov definitions
---

**NAV (net asset value):** Per-share value of a fund's holdings; the price mutual funds trade at daily.

**P/E ratio:** Share price divided by earnings per share; the most common valuation gauge.

**Portfolio:** The complete collection of an investor's holdings.

**Principal:** The original amount invested or loaned.

**Prospectus:** The legal document describing a fund's strategy, risks, and fees.

**Rebalancing:** Restoring a portfolio to its target allocation by trimming winners and adding to laggards.

**REIT:** Real Estate Investment Trust — a traded company owning income-producing property, required to pay out 90% of taxable income as dividends.

**Risk tolerance:** The loss an investor can endure — financially and emotionally — without abandoning the plan.

**RMD (required minimum distribution):** Mandatory annual withdrawals from tax-deferred retirement accounts starting at age 73.

**Roth IRA:** Retirement account funded with after-tax money; qualified withdrawals are tax-free.

**Securities:** Tradable financial instruments (stocks, bonds, fund shares).

**SIPC:** Insurance protecting brokerage customers (up to $500,000) if the broker fails — does not cover market losses.

**Standard deviation:** A statistical measure of volatility — how widely returns swing around their average.

**Stock:** A share of ownership in a corporation.

**Target-date fund:** An all-in-one fund that automatically de-risks as a chosen retirement year approaches.

**Ticker symbol:** The short code identifying a security (AAPL, VTI, SPY).

**TIPS:** Treasury securities whose principal adjusts with inflation.

**Total return:** Price change plus dividends/interest — the complete measure of performance.

**Volatility:** The size and frequency of price swings.

**Wash-sale rule:** IRS rule disallowing a tax loss if the same security is repurchased within 30 days.

**Yield:** Annual income (dividends or interest) as a percentage of price.
